# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal ops/support staff at Unico, running and monitoring catalog import jobs for pharmacy clients (Trier API, JSON file, or Alpha7 DB sources). Daily, multi-job workflow: launch an import, watch live progress and event logs, drill into individual line items by status (published, error, skipped). Power users, not end customers.

## Product Purpose

Internal admin tool for the Banco Unico product-catalog integration pipeline. Lets ops configure a source, kick off an import job, track real-time progress/logs, and audit per-item outcomes (EAN, classification, publish errors) without leaving the page. Success = an ops person can diagnose a failed import and re-run with confidence in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Clean, direct, professional. Linear/Vercel-dashboard register: monochromatic base, one strong accent used with purpose, tight typography, no decorative cruft. Confidence through restraint, not through colorful badges and shadows.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS template look: identical rounded cards everywhere, oversaturated status-badge palette, soft shadows on every surface, no real hierarchy.
- Dated raw-admin look: bare unstyled tables, no spacing rhythm, phpMyAdmin-era density without readability.

## Design Principles

- Operational clarity first: status, progress, and errors must be scannable at a glance during a live job.
- One accent, used deliberately: color encodes meaning (status/state), not decoration.
- Density without clutter: this is a data-heavy ops tool — embrace tables and real-time logs, but give them real hierarchy and breathing room instead of stacking identical cards.
- Calm dependability: muted, confident palette; motion only where it communicates state change (progress, live updates).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for live-update animations. No additional formal requirement beyond AA.
